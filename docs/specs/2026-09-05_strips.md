## Why
Mid-latitude and equatorial coverage is one narrow swath per pass,
rarely revisited. The natural product is not a time stack but a
library of independent strips, each a regular-grid field with a mask
and full geometry, queryable by latitude band, epoch, resolution, and
illumination, feeding spectra, structure functions, bicoherence, and
distribution-level model fitting.

## Definitions (normative)
- Unit rows: `frames_with_geo(mirror, orbits)` rows with `half == band`,
  `geo_ok`, `on_planet_frac >= 0.3`, `bore_emission <= 75`.
- Chunking: within each `seq_id`, sort by `start_time`; a chunk starts
  at the first row and a new chunk starts whenever the great-circle
  distance between the row's boresight and the chunk's first-row
  boresight exceeds 12 degrees, or the time gap to the previous row
  exceeds 120 s. Chunks with fewer than `--min-frames` (default 2) rows
  are skipped (counted in the summary).
- Grid per chunk: `local_ortho` centred on the chunk's boresight
  centroid (unit-vector mean of the on-planet boresights, converted
  back to planetocentric lat/lon), `km_per_px` = the resolution class
  nearest in log to the median of the chunk's `median_pixel_km`, from
  `RESOLUTION_CLASSES = (2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 30, 50, 70, 100, 150, 200, 300)`;
  extent: project every on-planet pixel (every 2nd line and sample of
  each frame's geometry) to the plane, take the bounding box, pad by 8
  px, and cap each side at 6000 px (report if capped). Row index
  increases with local north, column with local east.
- Strip content (xarray Dataset, dims `(y, x)`): `image` float32 (mean
  of contributing frames' bilinear samples, NaN where none), `valid`
  bool, `emission` float32 (min over contributing frames), `incidence`
  float32 (min), `n_frames` uint8 (contributing count), `frame_index`
  int16 (index into `product_ids` of the last contributing frame; -1),
  coords `x_km`, `y_km` (1-D), `lat`, `lon_east` (2-D float32),
  `local_time_h` (2-D float32 from the subsolar longitude at the
  chunk's mid-time, via `spiceypy.subslr` with the loaded kernels),
  `product_ids` (1-D str), `frame_times` (1-D datetime64). Attrs:
  `strip_id`, `orbit`, `seq_id`, `chunk_index`, `band`, `n_frames`,
  `time_start`, `time_end`, `time_mid`, `center_lat`, `center_lon_east`,
  `km_per_px`, `resolution_class` (same number, kept for filtering),
  `projection` (JSON of the grid `to_dict()`), `valid_frac`,
  `dayside_frac` (fraction of valid pixels with incidence < 90),
  `median_emission`, `lat_min`, `lat_max`, `lon_min_east`,
  `lon_max_east`, `lon_span_deg` (same arc rule as `geo.py`),
  `pole_inside`, `created_utc`, `software`.
- `strip_id = f"{orbit:02d}_{band}_{seq_time}_{chunk_index:02d}"` where
  `seq_time` is the `%Y%jT%H%M%S` part of `seq_id`. Files:
  `<mirror>/strips/orbitNN/<strip_id>.nc` (zlib, NaN fill).
- Library index `<mirror>/strips/strips.parquet`: one row per strip with
  every attr above plus `path` (relative to the mirror root), `rows`,
  `cols`. Rows for orbits not selected are kept; selected orbits are
  replaced (and their old files removed).
- `load_strips(mirror, *, orbits=None, band=None, lat_min=None, lat_max=None,
  time_min=None, time_max=None, resolution_max_km=None, min_valid_frac=None,
  dayside_min=None) -> pd.DataFrame` filters the index (latitude filter =
  overlap of [lat_min, lat_max] with [strip lat_min, lat_max]);
  `read_strip(mirror, strip_id | path) -> xr.Dataset`.
- CLI `strips --orbits SPEC --band M|L [--lat-band LO:HI] [--min-frames 2] [--jobs N] [--limit K]`
  (`--lat-band` filters unit rows by `bore_lat`; `--limit` caps chunks
  per orbit for testing). Parallelism: `spawn` workers per chunk with
  kernels cached per worker per orbit, as in `stacks.py`. A chunk whose
  computation raises is logged and skipped (never aborts the run);
  the summary reports skipped counts. Print per orbit: unit rows,
  chunks built, skipped, total pixels, wall time.
