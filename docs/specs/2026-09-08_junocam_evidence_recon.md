individual product/observation clearances under the current policy, and what
cannot be established. Report exact affected image/time intervals and timing,
exposure, summed-mode or colour-channel limitations for candidate passes.

evidence and retains final eligibility/scientific interpretation; executor
must not edit clearances or populate any observation as eligible.

Lead acceptance: inspect cited primary sources and verify each proposed
evidence claim against its supporting material before any policy change.

Judgment calls: quality screening requires evidence and measured signal;
metadata completeness, visual plausibility and a broad mission epoch each
leave different scientific questions unresolved.
